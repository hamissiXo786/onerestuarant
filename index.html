Rooted in Italy

Italian fire. Modern table.

Our kitchen is built around a single oak fire. Northern Italian technique meets the patience of the grill, so every plate carries a little smoke, a little warmth and a lot of care. We source close to home, make our pasta by hand each morning and let the flame do the rest.

The experience

Contemporary Italian, cooked over oak

Chef Lucia Ferrante cooked her way from Bologna to Florence before lighting the fire at Fiamma. Her menu is short, seasonal and built for sharing: honest ingredients, precise technique and the kind of warm hospitality that makes you stay for one more glass.
