Brand guide

Fiamma is a wood-fire Italian kitchen: warm, confident and a little theatrical. The identity borrows from the glow of embers and the rhythm of an Italian table. Deep reds, cream paper, tall serif capitals and a precise geometric sans.

01

Logo

A condensed, forward-leaning wordmark set in Antonio and skewed 9°, paired with a monogram: a hand-drawn F inside an oval, its stem lit by a single flame. Use the wordmark in navigation and signage, and the monogram where space is tight (favicons, embroidery, stamps).

02

Colour

Red leads. Oxblood and char add depth for evening moods, and cream gives the light sections their paper-like calm. Avoid pure white and pure black.

  • Fiamma Red#8C0A06Panels, headings on cream
  • Ember Red#A8100AButtons, active states
  • Oxblood#4A0905Secondary panels, footer
  • Deep Ember#2A0503Gradients, depth
  • Char#0E0C0BDark sections, header
  • Cream#F4E8DCText on red, light sections
  • Ink#241612Body text on cream

Cream on Fiamma Red has a contrast ratio of about 8:1, which passes WCAG AA and AAA for body text.

03

Typography

Two families do all the work. Cormorant Garamond, set in capitals, brings elegance and an editorial voice to headlines. Jost, a clean geometric sans, gives labels and body copy clarity and modern structure.
