Brand guide

One is where sophistication meets comfort. The identity comes straight from the room: brass letters glowing on green tiles, velvet booths, ring lights overhead, and champagne-coloured brunch posters with a little script flourish.

Logo

The wordmark is three monoline letters: a perfect circle O, an N and a mirrored E. Underneath, “Restaurant | Lounge” is set in small, widely spaced serif capitals. Use it in black on white (as on Instagram), in brass gold on dark or green, or in champagne on black. This drawn version is a stand-in for the official logo file.

Colour

Black and emerald set the evening mood; brass gold is the accent for buttons, rules and the logo; champagne carries the daytime brunch sections. Pool blue appears only as a glow.

Typography

Three voices, matching the brunch posters. Bodoni Moda capitals for headlines, Josefin Sans (a thin geometric close to the logo) for everything practical, and Pinyon Script used once per poster at most.
