# Reference study: "ENZO | Restaurant Branding Identity and Web Design" (Brickclay, Dribbble)

Source: https://dribbble.com/shots/27587971-ENZO-Restaurant-Branding-Identity-and-Web-Design
Studied from a 39-second screen recording of the shot (Dribbble blocks automated fetching).
These notes describe the *system*, which we borrowed. Fiamma does not reuse ENZO's name, logo, copy or photography.

## Positioning
- "ENZO Steakhouse & Bar" / "Enzo Ristorante Cucina Italiana": upscale, contemporary Italian.
- Taglines in the shot: "Italian Soul. Contemporary Expression.", "The Modern Italian Table",
  "Rooted in Italy, served with grace", "Modern evenings".
- The shot is framed as a **rebrand case study**: "Hero header: before" (large food image, no central
  message) vs. after, plus "What improved" checklists per section.

## Identity
| Element | What ENZO does |
|---|---|
| Wordmark | Condensed, slightly slanted, hand-cut sans caps in cream |
| Monogram | Slanted "E" inside an outlined oval; embroidered on chef jackets and aprons |
| Display type | **Denton**: high-contrast serif, light weights, ALL CAPS, tight leading |
| Text type | **ITC Avant Garde Gothic**: geometric sans for body, nav, tracked eyebrows |
| Colour | Signature red ≈ `#8A0001`, oxblood ≈ `#4D0000` → `#2A0000` gradients, near-black ≈ `#0C0C0B`, cream ≈ `#F3E6DC` |
| Buttons | Bright red, square corners, small tracked caps; text links end in "↗" |
| Photography | Low light, warm pendant lamps, dark glossy food, guests mid-moment, staff in uniform |
| Applications | Chef jacket, apron, napkin, table tents ("Table 21"), printed menu, business cards |

### Signature motifs
1. **Italian word stacks:** "SAPORE / TRADIZIONE / ELEGANZA / OSPITALITÀ / ITALIA" with a small English translation line.
2. **Letter-spaced grid:** "G U S T O / R I T U A L E / C A L O R E / P I A C E R E / I T A L I A" with a cut-out
   steak-on-a-fork photo breaking through the letters.
3. **Ring collage:** photo tiles around an empty central square.
4. **Staircase cascade:** photos fanned diagonally bottom-left → top-right (also used as a transition into the ring).
5. **Arched text** around the monogram ("ITALIAN SOUL", "Modern evenings").
6. **Ghost words** ("SPIRIT", "ITALY") huge and faint in the corners.

## Website structure (desktop, top → bottom)
1. **Nav** over the hero: wordmark · Menus, Gift card, Experiences ▾, Membership, Careers, Contact · "Make a reservation" + red "Order online".
2. **Hero:** full-bleed dark interior, centred serif caps "THE MODERN ITALIAN TABLE", red button + ghost link.
3. **Story split:** red text panel (eyebrow "ROOTED IN ITALY", headline, body at the bottom, "Discover full story ↗") | oxblood panel with ring collage.
4. **Chef / experience:** chef photo + red card "THE EXPERIENCE / CONTEMPORARY ITALIAN DINING", "View the menus ↗".
5. **Menu:** dark food photo, "CRAFTED WITH INTENTION. SERVED WITH GRACE.", cream cards Primi / Secondi / Dolci with red serif titles.
6. **Membership:** red band "ENZO ELITE MEMBERSHIP", "Explore memberships ↗".
7. **Private events:** cream section, "PRIVATE EVENTS / CATERING" + intro + "Plan your event ↗", horizontal cards (Corporate events, Private dining, Weddings & celebrations…).
8. **Experiences:** event cards, red date ("JULY 18 · 2026"), serif title, time, red "Get ticket ↗".
9. **Reserve your table:** dark band with CTA.
10. **Footer:** oxblood block inside a red frame: monogram, giant serif sign-off "L'ITALIA, SERVITA CON ELEGANZA.",
    newsletter (underlined input + arrow), Navigation / Opening hours / Contact (small uppercase labels), legal bar.

Mobile: the same sections stacked, with cream menu cards and full-width buttons ("mobile-first experience, clear navigation and focused actions make booking effortless").
